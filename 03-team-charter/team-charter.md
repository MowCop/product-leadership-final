# Team Charter: Fable Growth

> Module 3 · Set the Habits That Make a Team Faster

## 1. What We Own

> **We own:** The core Fable app, split between three PMs. PM 1 owns onboarding up to consent setup and the first completed practice in week 1, including the Later rebuild as a personalisation intake. PM 2 owns the in-app journey from week 1 onwards, including the pause and win-back screens. PM 3 owns the AI check-in layer, the tough-period flags it raises and, when it arrives, the co-pilot's product layer. PM 3 runs the guardrail review on every AI output, but Clinical gives the final sign-off. For check-ins and the streak alike, the owner decides what it does, and PM 1 or PM 2 decides where and when it appears.
>
> **Out of scope:** Growth owns the KR1 streak mechanics and experiment, the opt-in and flag-rate targets for KR3, control groups and reading the results. Growth also owns, with Finance, pricing, the terms of the pause offer and win-back targeting. Engineering owns notification delivery, sending at the right local time and the under-2s load time. Content owns the content library and podcast, and Design and Legal own the WCAG audit. Clinical decides what is safe, Privacy & Legal decide consent and data use, and AI/ML and Data build and evaluate the models. Therapist-matching, a social layer and Fable for Teams are on nobody's roadmap. Any request for them, including from the CEO, comes to Karl and is answered with the trade-off memo.
>
> **Joint calls:** The teams named in brackets must agree before anyone builds any of the following:
>
> - A new use of check-in data or wellbeing scores, or any change to consent (PM 3, Privacy & Legal, Clinical).
> - Any action triggered by a tough-period flag, such as the pause offer, win-back or outreach (PM 3, Clinical and the team acting on the flag).
> - Anything that pushes a user above 3 prompts or notifications a week, not counting the one daily check-in (PM 2, PM 3, Growth).
> - Any onboarding change that affects check-in opt-in or who counts as a high-engagement user (PM 1, PM 3, Growth).
> - Any experiment whose audience overlaps a live test, especially the day 60–90 users targeted by both the streak and pay-as-you-go pricing.

## 2. How We Decide

> **Who decides scope:** Within their own area, an owner decides scope alone if the change serves their KR and doesn't trigger a joint call. They log the decision in one line the same day. For a joint call, the PM whose KR is most affected proposes it in writing, and the others have 2 working days to object; no reply counts as agreement. Any change to a Now bet or its success target (30% weekly practice, 45% retention at 120 days) needs Karl's sign-off. Clinical and Privacy & Legal blocks can't be escalated, so the design has to change.
>
> **How cross-team conflicts escalate:** If an objection isn't resolved within 2 working days, any party posts it in the decision log with the options, the effect on each side's KR and a recommendation. Work on that item stops, but all other work carries on. Karl decides within 3 working days on conflicts inside the core product. Conflicts between product and Growth Karl decides jointly with the Head of Growth. If they disagree, the CEO makes the final call at the next weekly leadership review, with the reason logged.
